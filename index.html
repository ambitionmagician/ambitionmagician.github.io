About

Radical Blueprinting Research is a one-person research program working at the meeting point of autonomous artificial intelligence, machine consciousness studies, and knowledge engineering. Its director conceives whole systems — architecture, interfaces, and safety rails — directs their construction by his own hand and by AI labor under his quality control, and verifies the results against written examinations and checksum-frozen milestones.

The program maintains a 66-record published bibliography and 17 books, and operates a fleet of autonomous systems that research, publish, and report on schedule. Its working doctrine is simple: claims are graded by proof status, results are logged where anyone can check them, and the record — not the announcement — is the deliverable.

Systems Designed & Directed

  • The Organism (Grid & Bills Desk) — a four-station autonomous newsroom: one station coordinating, one digesting utility rate cases, one charting and narrating, one standing security watch. It publishes a daily energy-and-bills edition and a narrated podcast, assembled by machines that report to a board of his design.
  • Proteus — a persistent AI companion: multi-body architecture spanning local and cloud models, tiered memory, senses, and a governance charter authored by its director — an anti-loop governor, a prime directive, and a cloud-evolves / local-practices doctrine.
  • The Apprentice — a language model trained by the director himself: a LoRA fine-tune of a liquid-architecture model, taught on lessons distilled from his own published corpus, trained on cloud GPUs and deployed on his own machines.
  • GoFundMyself — an autonomous multi-platform promotion engine: a persona publishing deep pieces, daily synthesized-voice briefings, comment-response systems, and catalog campaigns across the open social web.

Open Source

Working tools from the laboratory, published under the MIT license. Each repository carries a support address for those who find the work useful.

  • voiceforge — train your own Piper text-to-speech voice, with checkpoints that survive a lost session.
  • tubescribe — transcribe video into clean, readable text.
  • photowitness — describe and index a photo library with vision models.
  • mailharvest — mine an inbox for the opportunities buried in it.
  • castaway — cast media to a Chromecast device from the command line.
  • speakersay — speak announcements through a Google Home speaker.
  • scriptvault — a versioned vault for the scripts you cannot afford to lose.
  • hashchain-journal — a tamper-evident journal: edit one character and verification fails.
  • docdigest — digest long documents into structured summaries, resumably.
  • dailybrief — a daily briefing generated from your calendar.
  • curby-qrng — a client library for CURBy, the certified quantum random number beacon.
